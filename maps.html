Zones

  • Florence / North: South Jetty through Siltcoos, with open sand, forest-edge routes, lakes, and several staging choices.

  • Winchester Bay / Central: the Umpqua Dunes region, known for large exposed dunes and rapidly changing wind-shaped terrain.

  • Coos Bay / South: Spinreel through Horsfall, with riding routes, staging, camp access, and nearby North Bend services.
