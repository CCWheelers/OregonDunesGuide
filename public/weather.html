DUNE WEATHER FIELD NOTES

What the numbers
mean on sand.

Weather changes traction, visibility, body temperature, and how quickly a simple recovery becomes serious.

01

Wind & gusts

Wind reshapes crests and can erase tracks. Strong crosswinds increase fatigue and make regrouping harder. Reduce speed before exposed ridges.

02

Rain & wet sand

Light rain can firm some sand, but storms reduce visibility and create colder conditions. Waterproof layers belong in every vehicle.

03

Fog & marine cloud

Dense fog removes visual landmarks quickly. Shorten loops, keep the group tight, and use offline navigation instead of following old tracks.

04

Cold exposure

Wind makes mild temperatures feel colder, especially after rain or sweat. Carry a dry insulating layer even on summer afternoons.
