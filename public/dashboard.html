What do these numbers mean?

Visitors are estimated distinct people. Visits are sessions, so one person may visit more than once. Engagement means a visit lasted longer than ten seconds, contained a key event, or included at least two page views.

Search impressions count times the site appeared in Google results. CTR is the percentage that became clicks. Position is the average ranking; a smaller number is better.
