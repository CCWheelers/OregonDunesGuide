The Fine Print

What we will and will not do.

  • No pop-ups, no full-screen ads, no video that plays on its own. Nothing that covers the page or blocks what a visitor came to read. This guide is useful because it is fast and clean. We are not trading that for ad revenue.
  • Everything is labeled. Sponsored placements say so plainly. Safety, rules, and permit information is never for sale at any price.
  • Keep your creative simple. Send a logo and a phone number. We handle the layout so it matches the site and loads fast.
  • 30 days notice to cancel on monthly plans. If you sell or close the business, we refund the months you have not used.
  • We will turn business away. If a placement would mislead a visitor about riding rules or safety, we will say no and tell you why.
  • Rates are reviewed once a year. Founding partners are exempt for the length of their lock.
